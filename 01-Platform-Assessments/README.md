# Platform Assessments

Initial evaluations of AI platforms under consideration for organisational use. Each assessment explains what the platform does, who uses it, the business problem it solves, its integrations and data flows, and an early view of benefits and governance considerations — the input leadership needs to decide whether a platform warrants deeper risk assessment.

**Contents**
- `Snowflake-Cortex.md` — Platform assessment of Snowflake Cortex, the native AI layer of the Snowflake Data Cloud.
