# Risk Assessments

Structured risk analyses for assessed platforms, covering security, privacy, compliance, third-party, data protection, human oversight, operational, and reputational risk. Each risk is rated for likelihood and impact and paired with a proposed mitigation and risk owner. These mitigations become the basis for the controls in `03-Control-Library`.
